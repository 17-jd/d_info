# Outreach Playbook — by Segment

How to open with each segment: the *reason to call*, what to *lead with*, and a
sample opening line. Pair this with each lead's `buying_trigger` and
`hardware_need` columns. Keep the first call short — book a visit/demo.

> Tone for Surat SMB owners: direct, value-first, local. Mention you're a
> Surat-based vendor with on-site service & AMC (response time beats price for
> most owners). Hindi/Gujarati rapport helps.

---

### Lab-Grown Diamond (CVD/HPHT) — Tier A
- **Why now:** new units & reactors opening constantly → full greenfield IT fit-outs.
- **Lead with:** GPU workstations for planning/grading software, reactor-control PCs that don't crash, NAS for designs, and **24×7 CCTV with remote view** for the owner.
- **Open:** *"You're setting up / expanding a CVD unit — I supply the planning workstations, the camera setup, and networking as one package with on-site AMC. Can I show you a sample config?"*

### Diamond & Bourse — Tier A
- **Why now:** firms opening new offices (esp. Surat Diamond Bourse) → seats + networking + heavy CCTV.
- **Lead with:** multi-floor networking, **high-density CCTV + biometric access** (security is everything here), planning workstations, servers.
- **Open:** *"For your new Bourse / Mahidharpura office — I do the complete cabling, cameras with access control, and the desktops. One vendor, one AMC."*

### IT & Software — Tier A/B
- **Why now:** hiring, new floors, refresh cycles, client security SLAs.
- **Lead with:** developer workstations, rack server + NAS, firewall + managed switches + Wi-Fi, fast turnaround on replacements.
- **Open:** *"You're hiring devs — I can do a workstation + dock standard build, plus your server and firewall, with same-day on-site support."*

### GIDC Manufacturing — Tier A/B
- **Why now:** capacity expansion, automation/Industry-4.0, new lines.
- **Lead with:** ERP/SAP servers + storage, rugged/industrial PCs, **plant-wide CCTV**, networking, biometric access, AMC.
- **Open:** *"For the new line/plant — I handle the ERP server, shop-floor PCs, the camera coverage and the network as a single AMC contract."*

### Healthcare — Tier A/B
- **Why now:** new wing/branch, new imaging machine, EMR/NABH digitisation.
- **Lead with:** HIS/EMR server + storage, PACS imaging workstations, reception/billing PCs, networking, CCTV, UPS-backed.
- **Open:** *"For your imaging/EMR rollout — I supply the PACS-grade workstations and the server/storage with backup, plus the camera and network."* (Ask for Administrator / IT Manager.)

### Education — Tier A/B
- **Why now:** new campus/building, lab upgrades, more seats, smart classrooms.
- **Lead with:** **bulk lab desktops**, lab server, campus Wi-Fi + networking, CCTV, interactive panels/projectors.
- **Open:** *"For your computer lab refresh / new block — I can quote bulk desktops, campus Wi-Fi, cameras and smart panels together, with annual AMC."* (Ask for Principal/Director/IT Head/Trustee.)

### Media / Video / Photo — Tier B
- **Why now:** 4K/8K upgrade, new studio, growing creator/wedding-film work.
- **Lead with:** GPU editing workstation / Mac Studio, **fast NAS + backup**, color-grade monitor, 10G networking.
- **Open:** *"Editing 4K is choking your machines — I build editing workstations and a shared NAS so your team isn't waiting on renders/exports."*

### Architecture & CAD/Engineering — Tier B
- **Why now:** more/larger projects, render upgrades, team growth.
- **Lead with:** RTX GPU workstations (AutoCAD/Revit/3ds Max/Lumion), large/dual monitors, project NAS + backup.
- **Open:** *"Your Lumion/Revit renders will fly on the right GPU workstation — I'll spec one to your software and add a project NAS. Quick demo?"* (Ask for Principal Architect/Partner.)

### Textile Manufacturing — Tier B
- **Why now:** capacity expansion, new sheds, ERP adoption, modernisation.
- **Lead with:** ERP/Tally server, shop-floor & billing PCs, **large-area CCTV**, networking, biometric attendance.
- **Open:** *"For the new shed — I cover the cameras across the floor, the billing PCs and the network, on one AMC so production never stops."*

### Jewellery — Tier B
- **Why now:** new showroom/branch, security mandates, festive scaling.
- **Lead with:** **high-density CCTV + access control** (lead here), POS/billing PCs, jewellery CAD workstations, networking.
- **Open:** *"For the new showroom — I do the full camera + access-control setup and the billing systems, with priority on-site service."*

### Textile Trade & Design — Tier B
- **Why now:** market complexes digitising, e-commerce push.
- **Lead with:** **bulk billing/POS desktops**, dense CCTV, complex-wide networking + Wi-Fi, textile-CAD for design studios.
- **Open:** *"For your shops in the market — I supply billing PCs, cameras and the network so billing and security are sorted before the season."*

### Hospitality & Real Estate — Tier B
- **Why now:** new hotel/property/project, renovation, sales galleries.
- **Lead with (hotel):** PMS server, front-desk PCs, **guest Wi-Fi**, CCTV. **(builder):** site CCTV, sales-gallery PCs, office IT.
- **Open (builder):** *"For your new site/gallery — I install the site cameras and the gallery/office PCs and network, ready before launch."*

### BPO / Logistics / Print — Tier B
- **Why now:** new seats/centre, client wins, new warehouse, press upgrade.
- **Lead with (BPO):** **bulk desktops/thin clients + headsets**, server, firewall, CCTV. **(Print):** pre-press color workstations, RIP server. **(Logistics):** office PCs, warehouse CCTV, barcode/handhelds.
- **Open (BPO):** *"Scaling seats? I can deliver matched desktops + headsets in bulk with a server and firewall, plus quick swap-outs on AMC."*

### Finance & Professional (CA / broker / co-op bank) — Tier B
- **Why now:** new branch, team growth, data-security/compliance upgrade.
- **Lead with:** reliable multi-seat desktops, **Tally/data server + NAS backup**, firewall, CCTV, UPS.
- **Open:** *"With client data on those machines — I'll set up a proper server with daily backup and a firewall, plus the desktops and cameras. AMC included."* (Ask for Partner/Proprietor.)

## Universal close
End every first contact the same way:
> *"Can I drop by for 15 minutes with a sample quote this week?"* — booking the
> visit is the win. Bring the `hardware_need` line as a ready mini-proposal.
