# Pre-Call Verification Protocol (kill the ghost leads)

Every lead is already **source-verified for existence** (it has a real
`source_url`). This is the fast routine to confirm the **contact** before you
spend a call — so no dial is ever wasted on a stale or wrong number.

## The 10-second confirm (do this for every lead before calling)

1. **Open the `source_url`.** Confirm the company name + that it's in/near Surat
   and in the stated category. (If the page is dead or clearly wrong → mark the
   lead `Rejected` and move on. This is rare but it's how you stay clean.)
2. **Get/confirm the phone.**
   - If the row already has a `phone`/`email`, cross-check it still appears on
     the company's site or Google Business listing.
   - If `contact pending`, search **"<company name> Surat"** on Google → use the
     Google Business panel number, or the company website's contact page, or its
     JustDial/IndiaMART listing.
3. **Confirm the decision-maker.** Search LinkedIn for the company → find the
   `contact_approach` role (Owner/Director, IT Head, Admin/Purchase, Principal,
   Hospital Administrator, etc.). Note the name if you can — opening with a name
   triples pickup.
4. **Stamp it.** In your CRM/sheet, set `verification_status = Confirmed
   <today's date>` and paste the working number. Now it's a real, dialable lead.
5. **DND/compliance check** (for bulk outreach): screen the number against
   India's DND / TRAI telemarketing rules; honour opt-outs.

## Tips for finding numbers fast (Surat-specific)

- **Diamond / textile firms** are reliably on **JustDial** and **IndiaMART** with
  phone numbers, even when their own site is thin.
- **Industrial (GIDC) units**: try the **IndiaMART seller page** and the unit's
  **GIDC/association listing**.
- **Hospitals, colleges, hotels**: the **official website footer / contact page**
  almost always has the reception + admin lines.
- **IT firms / agencies**: website contact page, **Clutch/GoodFirms** profile, or
  LinkedIn "About".
- A **Google Maps** listing for the business usually shows a verified phone and
  the exact area/landmark for a field visit.

## Why we do it this way

Faking 500 phone numbers would hand you exactly the ghost leads you don't want.
Instead each lead is anchored to a real source you can re-check in seconds, so
the list stays trustworthy months from now — and a junior teammate can run this
confirm pass in bulk before your calling day.
